Avionics section project — 2025

Maestro

A PyQt6 ground control application built for Monash UAS's search-and-rescue missions — one screen for live telemetry, mission management and AI-flagged detections of missing persons, fielded at this year's SUAS competition.

What it does

  • Telemetry dashboard — live aircraft state via MAVLink, bridged through MAVProxy to Mission Planner.
  • Live mission management — operators adjust and monitor missions mid-flight without leaving the GUI.
  • AI detection review — surfaces the vision model's detections of missing persons for operator confirmation.
  • Gimbal & camera control — drives the SiYi A8 Mini gimbal and a live RTSP video feed from the vision camera.

Hardest parts

  • Live video over RTSP — getting the vision camera's feed into the GUI without lag an operator could act on.
  • An incomplete gimbal SDK — the SiYi A8 Mini's SDK only exposed two of its three axes, so full gimbal control had to be built around that gap.
  • Deployment accuracy — offsetting the camera's geolocation error and compensating for wind drift, so humanitarian aid actually lands on the located patient.
